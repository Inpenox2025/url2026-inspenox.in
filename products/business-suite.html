Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about subscribing to and deploying Inspenox Business Suite.

Can I subscribe to only one module or do I need all modules?
You can subscribe to exactly the modules your business needs. If you only require Business ERP today, you subscribe to ERP alone. As your operations expand, you can unlock CRM, Marketing, Hospital ERP, or Asset DMS at any time with your existing master login.
How does Custom SSO benefit business owners with multiple branches?
Custom Single Sign-On gives branch managers and owners one master email and password. Once logged in, you can hop between ERP billing, CRM lead pipelines, WhatsApp marketing broadcasts, and hospital management from a universal top switcher bar without authenticating separately.
Does the Business ERP support both retail counter sales and corporate tax invoices?
Yes! Business ERP includes dedicated Dual POS billing: a high-speed B2C retail counter mode supporting barcode scanners, thermal receipts, and split payments, as well as a full B2B Wholesale mode generating GST tax invoices with buyer GSTIN, CGST/SGST/IGST splits, and PO references.
How does the Hospital ERP integrate Pharmacy and Diagnostic Labs?
Hospital ERP (icare) links clinical care directly with inventory and diagnostics. When an OPD or IPD doctor writes a prescription or orders lab investigations, the order instantly routes to the in-house pharmacy and lab desks. All dispensed medications, test fees, bed charges, and doctor fees are aggregated onto a single unified hospital bill.
How does the onboarding and feature setup work?
Onboarding is completely streamlined. When you register and select your required modules, our engineering team provisions your custom environment, imports your existing catalog or patient records, configures your branch manager roles, and delivers a fully functioning, ready-to-run suite.

What is INJARA LMS ?

INJARA LMS is a modern AI powered learning management system developed by Inspenox Pvt Ltd. It helps educational institutions deliver structured learning programs, coding challenges, assessments and placement preparation through a scalable cloud platform.

The platform integrates learning modules, AI assistance, analytics dashboards, coding practice environments and career readiness tools to create a complete digital learning ecosystem.

INJARA LMS for Colleges and Training Institutes

INJARA LMS is designed for educational institutions that want a scalable digital learning platform. The system supports online courses, live classes, coding assessments, placement preparation modules and skill development programs.

Institutions can manage thousands of learners through a centralized cloud based LMS infrastructure with analytics, leaderboards and automated testing tools.
